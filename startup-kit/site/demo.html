The 15-minute demo

Three things: the work, the pause, the receipts.

No recorded video — the demo is live, on your kind of work, in the same five beats every time. The prospect sees an AI employee do their kind of work, sees it stop and ask permission, and sees the audit trail.

  1. Beat 1 — the hire (2 min). We hire a Data Clerk in front of you with your website in its allowlist and the consent gate ON. You see the three settings: allowlist (your site only), consent gate ON, step cap. Allowlist means it can't wander; consent gate means nothing goes out without your click.
  2. Beat 2 — the work (5 min). The employee does your workflow — e.g. reads a form on your site, fills a sample order, verifies each field before typing. Never a task that isn't your workflow.
  3. Beat 3 — the pause (4 min). The task parks at awaiting approval. Nothing has been sent — it filled the form and stopped. This is the moment you stay in control, every time. You approve, or you don't; then we click Approve and it finishes. The gate is never turned off, even to "show it faster."
  4. Beat 4 — the receipts (3 min). We open the task's audit trail and scroll it slowly: every step in plain English — what it read, what it typed, what it asked permission for, what you approved. The employee that writes its own time sheet.
  5. Beat 5 — the offer (1 min). Free for 7 days on your real work. We set it up, you keep the audit trail. If it doesn't save you time, we pull it and you owe nothing.

Demo rules

What we will and won't do.

  1. Never demo a task that isn't your workflow.
  2. Never turn the consent gate off.
  3. Never claim a customer we don't have — "You'd be my first, which is why it's free," is stronger than a fake logo wall.
