How it works

  1. Kickoff — 30 minutes. You show (not describe) the repetitive work: the spreadsheet, the inbox, the form. Together we write the job description in your words — what the employee reads, where it acts, what it must ask permission for, what "done" looks like. We agree the allowlist explicitly (domains only) and book a daily 5-minute gate window for pilot days 1–7.
  2. We hire and train. Rules, permissions, allowlist, approval gate — set up for you. We deploy the sealed appliance, configure the consent gate ON, and seed the first real task list from what we watched. The audit trail writes from step one. Nothing for you to learn.
  3. Free 7-day pilot. Days 1–2, supervised start: we watch the first runs end-to-end; you watch the dashboard and approve parked submissions in the agreed window; each decision lands in the trail with your name on it. Days 3–5, steady state: scheduled morning runs, you approve at the gate unassisted, and a day-4 hours-saved readout from your trail becomes the conversion case — or the honest reason to stop.
  4. You decide. Day 6: full audit replay of your real work. Day 7: two options only — convert (setup + retainer per the rate card; founding terms if still open) or stop (appliance wound down, volume handed over, records yours). Silence never converts you; we ask, you decide. Keep it, or we pull it and you owe nothing.
