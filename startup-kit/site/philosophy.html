Philosophy

The employee asks. You decide. Everything is written down.

Six principles govern our product, sales, and operations. The constraints are the product.

Back to homeSee the demo
01

Consent before action

The AI pauses for human approval before anything is committed. The consent gate parks every form submission, booking, and outbound message; one click resumes it. There is no auto-approve toggle, and there never will be.

02

Everything is written down

Every step lands in a timestamped, plain-English audit trail. The audit log is the spine — the dashboard reads it, and the demo's final beat replays it.

03

Your data stays in your box

The employee runs in a sealed appliance on hardware you control: one container, one named data volume, one customer. Offboarding hands the volume over. We do not build a multi-tenant cloud.

04

Drafts only, always

Nothing sends without a human pressing send. The Outreach Assistant produces drafts; the transport refuses to act without explicit arming and an operator gate. The DNC list is enforced in code, pre-send.

05

Evidence or silence

We don't say anything we can't show. Findings reports carry evidence — screenshots, replayed steps — never adjectives. An empty result is reported as empty.

06

Small on purpose

Few roles, few customers, deep supervision. No self-serve signup, no marketing funnels beyond the site, no scaling talk before the founding five exist. Growth follows proof.

The iron rule

A template is sold only after it has done the work for us.

On our own operations — and passed a run on a real model. This is why the catalog is short and why every "shipped" badge is true. There are no exceptions, including for us.

What we refuse

  1. Auto-approve anything, even "just this once."
  2. Meter or surveil beyond the audit trail the customer owns.
  3. Hold customer data anywhere but the customer's appliance.
  4. Sell a role that hasn't passed the dogfood gate.
  5. Send anything without a human press of send — ours or theirs.
  6. Claim an outcome we haven't logged.
