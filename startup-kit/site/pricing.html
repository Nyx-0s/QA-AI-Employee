Quoting rules

How we quote — in writing.

  1. Quote the sheet, never improvise. Discounts exist only as the founding-five terms and the money-back guarantee.
  2. Pilot is free, always. If a pilot "needs" a paid component, the workflow is wrong — we fix the workflow, not the pitch.
  3. Scope moves are conversations, not invoices. A new source or task within the same role is handled in the retainer. A genuinely different role is quoted from this sheet.
  4. Money flow: setup invoiced at conversion, then monthly in advance, cancel any time. No contract term.
  5. Rate locks honored literally. A founding customer's rate stays for 12 months even as list prices rise.
  6. Refunds: same day, same rail, no forms. The audit trail is attached to the refund note.

Deliberately not offered: usage-metered, pay-per-outcome, per-seat, self-serve tiers, revenue share. Source: docs/pricing-model.md.
