# Outreach templates — the professional library

*Drafts for the operator to personalize, gate, and send. These are
templates, not autonomous sends — every message goes through the same
gates we sell (`sales/operator-runbook-approve-send.md`). Personalization
placeholders in `[brackets]` must be filled with evidence before a draft
can be approved; rule zero applies: no claim without a receipt.*

---

## Rules of use (binding)

1. **Drafts only.** Nothing here sends itself. Transport stays gated
   (`NUDGE-GATE`, `CA_SEND_ARMED=1`); DNC list checked by the transport
   and by you.
2. **≤5 sends/day** until domain warm-up completes
   (`sales/deliverability-baseline.md`).
3. **Personalize or discard.** A template sent with an unfilled
   placeholder is a broken draft — the pre-flight audit should never see
   one. One true, specific detail beats any amount of polish.
4. **No invented outcomes.** Numbers only from
   `sales/offer-sheet.md` (prices, pilot length, guarantee) or the
   customer's own audit trail. Never "our clients love us" — we don't have
   clients yet, and pretending otherwise is the one unforgivable error.
5. **One ask per message.** Reply, or a 15-minute slot. Never two.
6. **Log every send** in `sales/ledger.md` the same motion (evidence or
   silence).

## Reply snippets (use inside reply-playbook drafts, `sales/reply-playbooks.md`)

- **"How much?"** → "[Role]: [setup] + [monthly]/mo, month to month.
  Before any of that: a free 7-day pilot so the numbers are yours, not
  ours. Full sheet attached."
- **"Is my data safe?"** → "Short version: your employee runs in a sealed
  appliance on your hardware; we hand you the whole volume at offboarding
  and confirm in writing that no copy remains. One-pager attached —
  happy to walk your [IT person/counsel] through it."
- **"Send more info"** → "Faster to show: 15 minutes and you'll have seen
  the whole product — the work, the pause, the trail. [Times]. If you'd
  rather read first: [offer sheet] and [how-it-works page]."
- **"Not now"** → "Understood — I'll stop here. For the record: the offer
  doesn't expire, [season] might be the natural moment, and I'll check
  the calendar once more in [month]. Anything changes, you know where I
  am." *(Then: set the nudge date in the ledger — the system remembers so
  the human doesn't have to.)*

## Forbidden patterns (auto-reject in review)

- Any claim of results we haven't logged ("boosted revenue", "clients
  report…") — the company is pre-revenue; the site says so honestly.
- Fake urgency ("only 2 spots left") — founding terms are tracked in the
  ledger; if true, cite it, else omit.
- Attachments the recipient didn't expect beyond the QA report or offer
  sheet. No decks. No videos. Evidence only.
- More than one ask. More than ~150 words for Track A.
- Any send where the personalization line could apply to anyone else.
