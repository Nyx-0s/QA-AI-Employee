## What you're hiring

An **AI employee** — not software you operate. We deploy it, supervise it,
and keep it working. It:

- works only inside **domains you allowlist**,
- **pauses for a human approval before any form submission** — always,
- keeps a **plain-English audit trail** of every step it takes,
- runs in **your own sealed appliance** (your data never leaves your box),
- never sends an email or message on its own — drafts only, you send.
