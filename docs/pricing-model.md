# ConsentArk — pricing model & rate card

*The locked pricing decision: why fixed setup + monthly retainer, the
numbers, and every rule for quoting. Public version:
`startup-kit/site/pricing.html`; printable customer sheet:
`sales/offer-sheet.md`.*

---

## The decision

**Every ConsentArk employee is priced as: one-time setup fee + flat monthly
retainer.**

There is no third component. No usage meter, no per-seat fee, no contract
term, no platform percentage. If a deal seems to need one, it isn't a
ConsentArk deal.

## The locked numbers (founding cohort)

| Employee | Setup | Monthly | First-year total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Data Clerk | $500 | $300/mo | $4,100 |
| QA Analyst | $750 | $400/mo | $5,550 |
| Outreach Assistant | $500 | $300/mo | $4,100 |
| Scheduler capability | included | included | — |

- **Free 7-day pilot** on the customer's real work. We set everything up.
  The customer keeps every audit trail produced, whatever they decide.
- **30-day money-back guarantee** after conversion: if the employee doesn't
  clearly save hours, every dollar is refunded. The audit trail shows it
  either way — the customer never has to argue.
- **Founding five:** setup waived + rate locked for 12 months, in exchange
  for a candid testimonial and permission to reference the workflow
  (anonymized if preferred). When the five are taken, this line comes off
  the site and the sheet — never quietly extended.

## Why this shape (and why not the alternatives)

**The setup fee buys hiring.** Kickoff, job description, rules, allowlist,
consent-gate configuration, the sealed appliance, and training on the
customer's real workflow. It's paid once — like hiring a good employee.

**The retainer buys the work.** Operation, supervision, and upkeep by us.
Flat, because an employee doesn't bill by the task; and flat is the incentive
alignment: we profit most when the employee quietly does its job.

Rejected, with reasons:

| Alternative | Why rejected |
| --- | --- |
| Usage metered / pay-per-task | rewards busywork; adversarial to trust; unpredictable bills for small businesses |
| Pay-per-outcome | unsolvable attribution arguments; the audit trail helps but the haggling is the product's opposite |
| Per-seat SaaS | there is one "seat" (the employee); seat pricing would be theater |
| Self-serve tiers | deferred deliberately — pilots need us; tiering comes after proof |
| Revenue share | couples our income to numbers we don't control; misaligned with flat supervision |

## Quoting rules

1. **Quote the sheet, never improvise.** Discounts exist in exactly two
   forms: the founding-five terms and the money-back guarantee. Anything
   else is "let me confirm and come back to you" → record in
   `sales/audit-log.md`.
2. **Pilot is free, always.** If a pilot "needs" a paid component, the
   workflow is wrong — fix the workflow, not the pitch.
3. **Scope moves are conversations, not invoices.** A new source or task
   within the same role: handled in the retainer. A genuinely different
   role: quoted from the sheet.
4. **Money flow:** setup invoiced at conversion (day 0 after pilot), then
   monthly in advance, cancel any time. No card on file during pilots.
5. **Rate locks:** honored literally. A founding customer's $300 stays $300
   for 12 months even as list prices rise.
6. **Refunds:** same day, same rail, no forms. The audit trail is attached
   to the refund note — the receipt of what did (or didn't) get saved.

## Cost notes (internal)

- Delivery cost per customer = model usage inside their appliance + our
  supervision time. Supervision rituals are documented and scripted; model
  cost is per-appliance and passed through at cost where a customer runs
  their own key.
- The demo sandbox runs on the deterministic mock backend: zero marginal
  cost per demo.
- Founding-cohort economics: setup revenue waived on purpose — it is the
  marketing budget, converted into testimonials.

## Where pricing goes next (and only then)

After the founding five convert and two quarters of audit-trail evidence
exist: raise setup on new customers (waived only for founding), add roles
by demand (`startup-kit/README.md` gates), and consider a second tier only
if a repeated customer shape appears in the evidence — never from
curiosity.
