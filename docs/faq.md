# ConsentArk — FAQ (sales-ready answers)

*Every answer below is safe to say verbatim to a prospect: it matches the
site, the offer sheet, and what the product actually does. Expanded from the
pricing FAQ; here for calls, replies, and objections.*

---

## What exactly is an "AI employee"?

Not a chatbot and not a tool you operate. It's a worker we hire out: it has
a job description (agreed at kickoff), an allowlist (only the sites you
name), an approval gate (it stops and asks before anything is submitted or
sent), and an audit trail (every step written down in plain English). We
deploy it, supervise it, and keep it working. You never touch software.

## How is this different from using ChatGPT or an automation tool?

Chatbots improvise and answer; your employee executes a defined job inside
fences you set. Automation tools move data on triggers you configure and
trust blindly; ours pauses for a human decision at the exact moment
something is committed, and writes down everything. The difference isn't
the AI — it's the structure around it.

## Will it send emails or messages on its own?

No. That is a hard rule with no exceptions, including for us. The Outreach
Assistant produces drafts in your voice; a human presses send, every time.
Our own sales outreach runs through the same rule — you can see the gates
we use on ourselves.

## Where does my data live?

In a sealed appliance on hardware you control — one container, one data
volume, one customer. We don't run a multi-tenant cloud and don't want your
data in one. Offboarding means we hand you the volume and confirm in
writing that no copy remains anywhere.

## What can go wrong, and what happens then?

The audit trail is the shared record of everything, so "what went wrong"
has an answer in plain English, with the step where it happened. Root cause
and the fix are delivered to you the same way. The gate makes the worst
categories of error (unapproved submissions, sends) structurally
impossible — not unlikely, impossible.

## How long until it's actually working?

Pilot live within a week of your yes: a 30-minute kickoff, we deploy and
train the same week, days 1–2 are supervised, by day 3 it's in its morning
rhythm. If setup would take longer than that for your workflow, we tell
you at the kickoff instead of discovering it during the pilot.

## What does the pilot cost, and what's the catch?

Nothing, and there's no catch: free 7-day pilot on your real work, we set
everything up. You keep every audit trail it produced whether you convert
or not. The catch, stated plainly: we're good at the workflows we've
proven — data entry, site QA, follow-up drafting — and we'll tell you if
yours isn't one of them before the pilot starts.

## What happens after the pilot?

Two options, both clean: keep the employee (setup + monthly retainer per
the rate card), or we wind it down and hand you the volume. Silence never
converts you; we ask, you decide.

## Is the monthly price a subscription?

It's a retainer — it pays for the employee's operation and supervision,
not access to a tool. Cancel any time; no contract term, no early-termination
fee. The trail of everything it did is yours to keep.

## What does the setup fee pay for?

The hiring: kickoff, job description, allowlist and gate configuration, the
appliance, and training on your real workflow. One-time. Adding a source or
task later within the same role is a conversation, not a new fee.

## Do you offer pay-per-outcome or usage pricing?

No — deliberately. Metered pricing rewards busywork and makes your bill a
puzzle. A flat retainer means our incentive is that the employee genuinely
saves you hours, and the audit trail shows whether it does.

## What if I'm not satisfied?

The 30-day money-back guarantee after conversion: if the employee doesn't
clearly save you hours, every dollar comes back — the audit trail shows it
either way, so there's nothing to argue. During the pilot it's even
simpler: you haven't paid anything.

## What are the "founding customer terms"?

For the first five businesses: setup fee waived, your rate locked for 12
months, in exchange for a candid testimonial and permission to reference
your workflow (anonymized if you prefer). Ask whether slots remain; when
they're gone, they're gone — never quietly extended.

## Who is this NOT for?

Anyone who wants full autonomy with no human in the loop, needs the
employee to touch sites outside an explicit allowlist, or wants to skip the
pilot and buy in bulk. That's a different product from a different company.

## Can I see it before talking to anyone?

Yes — the live demo runs the same choreography every time: extraction
with evidence, the pause at the gate, one approval, and the audit replay.
Start at c0nsen7.xyz/demo, then book the 15-minute version on your own
workflow when it makes sense.
